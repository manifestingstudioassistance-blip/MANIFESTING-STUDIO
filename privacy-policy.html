Privacy Policy — Manifesting Studio Last updated: September 24, 2026 Manifesting Studio (“the App”) respects your privacy. By using Manifesting Studio, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy. 1. Data Controller The data controller for Manifesting Studio is: MANIFESTING STUDIO Email: manifestingstudioassistance@gmail.com 2. Information We Collect Manifesting Studio is designed to minimize the collection of personal information. Journal The App allows you to create personal Journal entries, including: thoughts; goals; affirmations; intentions; manifestation notes; other text you choose to enter. Journal content is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to a Manifesting Studio account or stored on our servers. We do not have access to your Journal entries. Voice Recordings Manifesting Studio may allow you to record your own voice for creating personalized subliminal audio. Voice recordings and generated audio files are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to Manifesting Studio servers. We do not have access to your recordings unless you explicitly use a feature that requires sending audio to a third-party service. Subliminals and Other Content The App may allow you to create and save: affirmations; subliminal audio; personalized audio; selected background sounds; audio settings and preferences. These files are stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated within the App. 3. Artificial Intelligence Manifesting Studio may use Claude by Anthropic to provide certain AI-powered features. When you choose to use an AI feature, information that is necessary to provide that feature may be sent to Anthropic. For example, if you enter text into an AI-powered feature, that text may be transmitted to the AI service so that it can generate the requested response. AI features are optional. You can choose not to use them. AI provider: Anthropic / Claude For information about how Anthropic handles data, please consult Anthropic’s current privacy documentation. 4. How We Use Information Information may be processed to: provide the App’s features; generate AI responses when requested; create personalized subliminal audio; process voice recordings when required for a feature; save your Journal and other content locally on your device; maintain the security and functionality of the App; diagnose technical problems and improve the App. 5. Manifesting Studio does not automatically upload this content to its own servers. If you uninstall the App or delete its local data, locally stored content may be permanently deleted. Users should make their own backups if they wish to preserve their content. 7. Third-Party Services The App may use third-party services necessary for certain functionality.
