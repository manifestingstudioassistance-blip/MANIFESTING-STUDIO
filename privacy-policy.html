Privacy Policy — Manifesting Studio

Last updated: September 24, 2026

Manifesting Studio (“the App”) respects your privacy. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the App and how that information is handled.

By using Manifesting Studio, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Data Controller

The data controller for Manifesting Studio is:

MANIFESTING STUDIO
Email: manifestingstudioassistance@gmail.com

2. Information We Collect

Manifesting Studio is designed to minimize the collection of personal information.

Journal

The App allows you to create personal Journal entries, including:

  • thoughts;
  • goals;
  • affirmations;
  • intentions;
  • manifestation notes;
  • other text you choose to enter.

Journal content is stored locally on your device and is not uploaded to a Manifesting Studio account or stored on our servers. We do not have access to your Journal entries.

Voice Recordings

Manifesting Studio may allow you to record your own voice for creating personalized subliminal audio.

Voice recordings and generated audio files are stored locally on your device and are not uploaded to Manifesting Studio servers.

We do not have access to your recordings unless you explicitly use a feature that requires sending audio to a third-party service.

Subliminals and Other Content

The App may allow you to create and save:

  • affirmations;
  • subliminal audio;
  • personalized audio;
  • selected background sounds;
  • audio settings and preferences.

These files are stored locally on your device unless otherwise stated within the App.

3. Artificial Intelligence

Manifesting Studio may use Claude by Anthropic to provide certain AI-powered features.

When you choose to use an AI feature, information necessary to provide that feature may be sent to Anthropic. For example, text entered into an AI-powered feature may be transmitted to the AI service so it can generate the requested response.

AI features are optional. You can choose not to use them.

AI provider: Anthropic / Claude

For information about how Anthropic handles data, please consult Anthropic’s current privacy documentation.

4. How We Use Information

  • provide the App’s features;
  • generate AI responses when requested;
  • create personalized subliminal audio;
  • process voice recordings when required for a feature;
  • save your Journal and other content locally on your device;
  • maintain the security and functionality of the App;
  • diagnose technical problems and improve the App.

5. Account Information

Manifesting Studio currently does not require users to create an account. We therefore do not collect account credentials, passwords, or account profile information.

6. Local Storage

Journal entries, voice recordings, subliminals, and other user-created content are designed to remain on the user’s device. Manifesting Studio does not automatically upload this content to its own servers.

If you uninstall the App or delete its local data, locally stored content may be permanently deleted. Users should make their own backups if they wish to preserve their content.

7. Third-Party Services

The App may use third-party services necessary for certain functionality.

Principal AI service: Anthropic — Claude

Third-party services may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

8. Data Sharing and Sale

Manifesting Studio does not sell your personal information. We do not share your Journal entries, voice recordings, or personally created content with other companies for advertising purposes.

Information may be transmitted to third-party service providers when necessary to provide a feature that you have chosen to use, such as an AI feature.

9. Data Security

We take reasonable technical measures to protect information processed by the App. Because personal content is stored locally, its security also depends on the security of your device. We recommend using a device passcode and keeping your operating system up to date. No electronic storage system can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

Depending on your location and applicable law, you may have rights regarding your personal information, including the right to access, correct, delete, restrict processing of, object to processing of, withdraw consent for, and request portability of personal data where applicable.

Because most user-created content is stored locally on your device and is not accessible to us, you can generally manage or delete that content directly through the App or your device.

For privacy-related requests, contact manifestingstudioassistance@gmail.com.
